Jet Note 3.9.1 — Composer Header Safe-Area Fix

Fixed New Post top-bar vertical collapse introduced by 3.9.0 geometry alignment.

Root cause:
- The composer header was shifted down by --safe-top in layout.css.
- The same --safe-top was also added to the header height, padding, title position, and button position in pages.css.
- This counted the status-bar safe area twice and made the top bar abnormally tall.

Fix:
- The New Post screen now owns the safe-area padding.
- The header itself is exactly --jet-toolbar-height tall.
- Header title and buttons are positioned only inside that toolbar height.
- The 3.9.0 horizontal geometry remains unchanged: New Post is centered, Post shares the tool-column axis, and Cancel mirrors Post across the page center.

No publishing, media, tool mapping, scrollbar, or delete-confirm behavior was changed by this patch.
